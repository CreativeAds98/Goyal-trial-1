About Goyal Group

A Legacy of Trust &
Entrepreneurial Vision

Founded over seven decades ago, the Goyal Group has grown from a pioneering construction materials enterprise into a ₹750+ crore diversified conglomerate. Led by Chairman & MD Rajendra Prasad Goyal and his son Vikas Goyal, the group spans five high-growth sectors — cementing its status as one of India's most trusted business families.
