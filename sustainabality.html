Our Philosophy

Sustainability Isn't a
Programme — It's a Promise.

At Goyal Group, sustainability is not a department or an annual report. It is the lens through which every business decision is made — from the suppliers we choose to the warehouses we build, from the communities we operate in to the materials we put into India's buildings.

We believe that every brick laid, every pipe installed, and every home delivered carries an obligation — to the people who will live in it, the communities around it, and the planet that supports us all.

"We are not just building structures. We are building a legacy — one that future generations will inherit. That responsibility guides everything we do."
— Vikas Goyal, Partner & Chief Orchestrator

Key Initiatives

From Commitment
to Action

Our sustainability commitments are backed by concrete, on-ground programmes underway across all five business verticals today.

01
Solar-Powered Warehousing
We have installed solar arrays across our flagship 2 lakh sq.ft. warehouse in Bengaluru, generating clean energy to power our forklifts, lighting, and cooling systems. The project reduces grid dependency by 35% and is being expanded to all future facilities.
35% Grid Reduction Solar PV Arrays 2L sq.ft. Facility Expanding to All Sites
02
50,000 Trees — Greening Karnataka
In partnership with local forest departments and NGOs, Goyal Group has planted over 50,000 native trees across Karnataka. We match every major contract signed with a tree-planting pledge, making every deal a contribution to India's green cover.
50K+ Trees Planted Native Species Karnataka-Wide NGO Partnership
03
Green Catalogue — Eco-Certified Products
We have curated a dedicated "Green Catalogue" within our construction B2B offering — featuring water-efficient fittings, low-VOC tiles, recycled-content building materials, and energy-saving products from certified manufacturers. Now used by 30+ developer clients.
500+ Green SKUs 30+ Builder Clients Eco-Certified Low-VOC
04
EV Fleet Transition
Goyal Group's logistics and last-mile delivery fleet is being progressively converted to electric vehicles. Phase 1 covers 20% of our Bengaluru delivery fleet with 3-wheelers and light EVs. Phase 2 targets 50% EV adoption by 2027 across all delivery operations.
Phase 1: 20% EV 2027 Target: 50% 3-Wheelers + LCVs Zero Emission Delivery

Community Impact

Rooted in Communities.
Growing with India.

Beyond our business operations, Goyal Group is committed to the wellbeing of the communities that surround us — investing in education, health, livelihoods, and infrastructure.

Education Scholarships
Annual merit-cum-need scholarships for children of workers in our supply chain — covering school fees, books, and uniforms for underprivileged students.
📚 500+ students supported annually
Clean Drinking Water
Water purifier installations in 12 villages and urban settlements near our Bengaluru facilities — providing safe drinking water to families who previously relied on contaminated sources.
💧 12 villages · 5,000+ beneficiaries
Vocational Skill Training
Partnership with local ITIs to train youth in plumbing, electrical fitting, tile laying, and warehouse operations — creating job-ready workers for the construction sector.
🔧 1,200+ youth trained in 3 years
Healthcare Access
Mobile health camps organised quarterly near our warehouse and operational hubs — providing free consultations, medicines, and health check-ups to workers and their families.
🏥 4,000+ consultations annually
