About Goyal Group

A Legacy of Trust &
Entrepreneurial Vision

Founded decades ago, the Goyal Group has grown from a pioneering construction materials enterprise into a diversified conglomerate spanning five high-growth sectors. Led by Chairman & MD Rajendra Prasad Goyal and his son Vikas Goyal, the group commands a turnover exceeding ₹750 crores while maintaining its core promise of quality, reliability and long-term partnerships.
