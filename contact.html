FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to things our partners and clients most often ask about working with Goyal Group.

What types of construction materials does Goyal Group supply?
We supply the full spectrum — cement (UltraTech, ACC, Ambuja), tiles & flooring, bathroom fittings (Jaquar, Kohler), CPVC/PVC pipes (Astral, Supreme), sanitary ware, adhesives, grouts, steel, TMT bars and much more. We are a single-source B2B partner for all your construction material needs.
Do you offer bulk pricing for large construction projects?
Absolutely. Goyal Group specialises in high-volume B2B supply. We offer structured volume pricing, credit terms, and dedicated account management for developers, contractors and EPC companies. Please reach out via the form above or call Vikas Goyal directly for a tailored quote.
Which cities and states does Goyal Group operate in?
Our headquarters and primary operations are in Bengaluru, Karnataka. Through our distribution and logistics network, we serve clients across 200+ locations Pan-India, with particular strength in South India — Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
How can I become a distribution partner or dealer?
We are always looking for reliable distribution and dealership partners who share our commitment to quality and customer service. Select "Partnership" in the inquiry form above, fill in your details, and our business development team will get in touch to discuss terms, territories and requirements.
What is the typical lead time for bulk material orders?
With our 2 lakh+ sq. ft. warehouse and deep supplier relationships, most in-stock items are available for same-day or next-day dispatch within the Bengaluru region. For out-of-stock or customised orders, lead times typically range from 3–7 business days depending on the product category and volume.
Are there career opportunities at Goyal Group?
Yes! With 25,000+ people across our group and rapid expansion across five sectors, we regularly hire in sales, logistics, operations, tech, finance and management. Send your CV and a brief note to vikas@thegoyalgroup.com with the subject line "Career Opportunity" and our HR team will review it.
