The Goyal Group was not built overnight. It was built conversation by conversation, delivery by delivery, trust by trust — over decades of unwavering commitment to the builders who raise India's skylines.

"Quality is not what we offer. It is who we are. Every brick, every pipe, every tile that leaves our warehouse carries our family name — and that means everything."
— Rajendra Prasad Goyal, Chairman & MD
The Beginning

Rajendra Prasad Goyal founded the group with a simple but powerful belief: that India's builders deserved a supply partner they could count on unconditionally. Starting with construction materials in Bengaluru, he built deep relationships with contractors, architects and developers at a time when reliable supply chains were hard to come by.

Growing the Foundation

Through the 1990s and 2000s, as Bengaluru transformed into a technology and real estate powerhouse, Goyal Group grew with it. The group became the preferred B2B supplier for India's most respected developers — Prestige Group, Brigade Group, Sobha Developers — earning a reputation for on-time delivery, product quality, and exceptional service that no competitor could match.

A New Generation of Growth

The arrival of Vikas Goyal as Partner and Chief Orchestrator brought fresh energy and bold diversification. Under his leadership, the group expanded into automotive distribution, e-commerce, real estate development, and warehousing — while the core construction B2B business continued to scale. Today, Goyal Group is a ₹750+ crore conglomerate spanning five high-growth verticals, 200+ locations across India, and over 25,000 people.

Where We're Headed

The Goyal Group is not resting. With infrastructure investment in India at an all-time high and digital commerce reshaping supply chains, we are positioned at the intersection of every major growth trend. Our vision: to be the most trusted diversified business group in India — across industries, across geographies, and across generations.

Our Journey

Seven Decades of Milestones

From a single trading operation to a diversified national conglomerate.

1950s
The Foundation is Laid
R.P. Goyal establishes the first trading operation in Bengaluru, supplying construction materials to the city's early builders with a focus on quality and reliability.
1970s–1980s
Expansion Across Product Lines
Growth into tiles, bathroom fittings, pipes and sanitary ware. Partnerships formed with leading manufacturers like UltraTech, Jaquar, and Astral.
1990s
Prestige & Brigade Partnerships
Goyal Group becomes the preferred supply partner for Prestige Group and Brigade Group — India's premier real estate developers — cementing its position as the #1 B2B construction supplier in Karnataka.
2000s
Bengaluru's Building Boom
As Bengaluru becomes India's tech capital, demand for construction materials surges. Goyal Group scales operations, expands warehouse infrastructure, and deepens its builder network.
2010s
Next-Gen Leadership Joins
Vikas Goyal joins as Partner & Chief Orchestrator, bringing entrepreneurial energy and a digital-first vision. The group begins diversifying beyond construction materials.
2020s
₹750 Cr+ Diversified Conglomerate
Automotive distribution, e-commerce, real estate and warehousing verticals launch and scale. Group turnover crosses ₹750 crore across 5 sectors, 200+ locations, and 25,000+ people.
TODAY & BEYOND
Building the Future of India
With infrastructure at record highs and digital commerce reshaping supply chains, Goyal Group is positioned for its next phase of growth — Pan-India expansion, tech-driven logistics, and new sector partnerships.
